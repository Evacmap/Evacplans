About Us

Signage that gets people out safely, first time, every time

Evac Maps is a dedicated evacuation-planning studio. We work 100% remotely with facility managers, architects and safety officers around the world, turning floor plans into evacuation maps and escape route signage that people can actually read under pressure — clear, compliant, and delivered without the cost or delay of an on-site visit.

  • Licensed reviewers check every plan against current fire code before it ships.
  • In-house design team — no outsourced drafting, one point of contact start to finish.
  • Fast turnaround — most projects delivered in 5–10 business days.
  • Worldwide coverage — fully remote design process, wherever your building is located.
